# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in MHDDoS to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or preferred tooling.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and technical. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith, then ask for evidence.
- Keep discussions on topic.
- Respect scope and authorization boundaries. Do not help with attacks on third parties.
- Accept a maintainer decision once it is made, or argue it with new evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sharing credentials, private keys, or target data.
- Encouraging or assisting attacks on systems without permission.
- Spam, repeated off-topic posts, or deliberate disruption.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any project space.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through a private channel or a GitHub report. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, or block accounts.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific code of conduct, not a legal document. It is inspired by common open-source standards such as the Contributor Covenant.
