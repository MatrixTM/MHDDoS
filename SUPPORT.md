## Questions

Use [Discussions Q&A](https://github.com/MatrixTM/MHDDoS/discussions/categories/q-a) for setup help and usage questions.

## Bugs

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/MatrixTM/MHDDoS/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with the command, environment, `DOCTOR` output, and the full error.

## Feature requests

Open a [feature request](https://github.com/MatrixTM/MHDDoS/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). For a new attack method, open a discussion first.

## Security

Report vulnerabilities privately through [Security Advisories](https://github.com/MatrixTM/MHDDoS/security/advisories/new). Do not use a public issue.

## What not to ask

Do not ask for help attacking a third-party target, for working proxy lists with credentials, or for bypasses against a specific live service. Those requests will be closed.
